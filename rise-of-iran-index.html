What We Are

Rise of Iran Initiative is an independent, civic-research initiative currently in formation. Its purpose is to help build the intellectual and civic infrastructure for a democratic transition in Iran.

The initiative is currently unincorporated and is led by Ali Ghobadi — Founder & Project Leader. The project is at the stage of building its initial structure, producing its first research outputs, and forming a collaborative network of experts.

Long-Term Vision

The initiative's long-term vision is to help shape an Iran where:

  • The rule of law and equal rights for all citizens are guaranteed;
  • Civil institutions and expert networks can operate independently;
  • Major decisions are made through public participation and democratic processes;
  • Public policy is developed based on research, expertise, transparency, and accountability.

Expert Network

The initiative intends to form an initial network of real experts who can contribute to research, review documents, and provide specialized analysis.

The network is currently in the process of formation. No individuals are listed as members or affiliated experts until their participation has actually been established.

Phase One — 6 Months

Objective: Establish the project's first verifiable track record through research outputs and an initial expert network.

Activities

  1. Prepare and publish 3 to 5 documented reports or analyses.
  2. Launch an official website and public archive.
  3. Form an initial network of 5 to 10 real experts.
  4. Develop initial processes for research collaboration, document review, and public dissemination.

Proposed Budget

€10,000 for the six-month phase.

Intended for direct and essential project costs such as research, translation and editing, website design and launch, communication and digital tools, and specialized implementation services. Potential legal registration costs will be assessed and funded separately.
